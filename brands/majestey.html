The crown emblem

What we did

Identity

The crown emblem, an embossed one-colour crown, and the MAJESTEY / LONDON lock-up.

Monogram & prints

A crown monogram repeat in forest, sage and butter, plus all-over and placement prints for the range.

Packaging & trims

Poly mailers, a sage gift box and tissue, plus hang tags, neck and wash-care labels, and a Ventilatech tag for Majestey Sport.

Sketches & tech packs

Flats and style-coded tech packs (LMP polos, LMJ joggers) with callouts, references and Pantones.

From sketch to sample

Every style starts as a flat with the callouts the factory needs: collars, tipping, plackets, embroidery and tapes. Swipe through six from the 2025 range.
