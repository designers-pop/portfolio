The crown emblem

What we did

Identity

The crown emblem, an embossed one-colour crown, and the MAJESTEY / LONDON lock-up.

Monogram & prints

A crown monogram repeat in forest, sage and butter, plus all-over and placement prints for the range.

Packaging

Branded poly mailers, a sage gift box, tissue and seal, so the brand arrives the same way it's worn.

Tech packs

Structured, style-coded tech packs for the polos and tees, replacing scattered versions and back-and-forth.
