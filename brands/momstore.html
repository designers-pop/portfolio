What we did

Range design

Seasonal boards for infant rompers, kids’ puffers, hoodies, sweatshirts and trackpants, with colourways for every style.

Tech packs & specs

Flats, construction callouts, print placement, Pantones and graded measurements, from 0 months to 10 years and maternity S–3XL.

Strike-offs & QC

Every print strike-off and sample checked against the last production, with clear notes and sizes back to the factory.

Sourcing & production

Fabrics, zips, pullers and labels sourced, then sampling and bulk production run season after season.
